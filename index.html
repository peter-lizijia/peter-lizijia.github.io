About

About me

I am an MSE student in Data Science at the University of Pennsylvania, working with Prof. Jiatao Gu in GMLR, GRASP Lab. Previously, I studied Mathematics with Applied Mathematics at the University of Nottingham and the University of Nottingham Ningbo China.

My research interests span world models, visual representation learning, video models, and image generation. I am interested in learning generalizable visual structure and dynamics for prediction, planning, and control.

I am seeking PhD opportunities starting in Fall 2027. Please feel free to get in touch if our research interests align.

Selected publication

CoRL 2026 LEAP Workshop Workshop

Normalizing Flows are Capable World Models

Zijia Li*, Yong-Hyun Park*, Shusheng Yang, Jiatao Gu

Workshop version accepted. Full manuscript under review. * Equal contribution.

NFWM jointly learns an invertible visual encoder and action-conditioned probabilistic dynamics through exact maximum likelihood. The model supports latent-space planning, policy learning in imagination, and observation generation, using information-preserving representations and distributions over possible futures.

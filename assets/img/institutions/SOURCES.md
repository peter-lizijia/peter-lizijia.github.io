# Institution logos

These marks identify the institutions listed in Zijia Li's education and research experience. They remain the property of their respective institutions and are not covered by the site's template license. Their presence does not imply institutional endorsement of this personal website.

- `penn.png`: original PNG from [Penn Brand Standards](https://branding.web-resources.upenn.edu/logos-and-branding/download-penn-logos), [source image](https://branding.web-resources.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/2022-03/UniversityofPennsylvania_Shield_RGB-2.png). Original colors and transparent canvas retained.
- `zhejiang.png`: original 200px PNG embedded in the [Zhejiang University website icon](https://www.zju.edu.cn/_upload/tpl/0b/bf/3007/template3007/static/media/icon_mlogo.4f71be0a36fc2a4abfa7.svg). The base64 image was extracted without modifying its pixels. [Official logo description](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/2023/0323/c74903a2732011/page.htm).
- `nottingham.png`: transparent castle mark from [StickPNG's University of Nottingham thumbnail](https://www.stickpng.com/img/icons-logos-emojis/russell-group-universities/university-of-nottingham-thumbnail), [source image](https://assets.stickpng.com/images/626ba4f71dc4c3b537787e24.png). [University logo guidance](https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CorporateMarketing/Services/Service-Details/Logos.aspx).

Downloaded November 5, 2026. Images are stored locally to avoid third-party image requests from visitors' browsers. CSS preserves their aspect ratios and accounts for transparent margins; the graphics have not been redrawn or recolored.

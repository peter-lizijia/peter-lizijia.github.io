Normalizing Flows are Capable World Models

Zijia Li*, Yong-Hyun Park*, Shusheng Yang, Jiatao Gu

CoRL 2026 LEAP Workshop

Workshop version accepted. Full manuscript under review. * Equal contribution.

NFWM jointly learns an invertible visual encoder and action-conditioned probabilistic dynamics through exact maximum likelihood. The model supports latent-space planning, policy learning in imagination, and observation generation, using information-preserving representations and distributions over possible futures.
